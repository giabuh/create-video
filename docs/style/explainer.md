# Explainer style bar

Source: `reference/explainer/sao-viet-brand-film.mp4` (1920x1080, 2:34; contact sheets beside it). It is the quality bar, not a template to copy.
Read before writing any `explainer` video. Facts come from `brands/<brand>/facts.md`, never from this file or the reference.

## Principles
- **Real footage is the backbone; motion explains on top of it.** Classes, students, campuses, certificates stay on screen most of the time. Motion annotates footage, frames it in a card, or sits beside it. Pure-motion pages are for ideas no footage can show (a number, a curve, a map) and stay short.
- **One idea = one visual.** Each spoken idea gets one picture that makes it understood at a glance. No idea reuses the visual of the one before.
- **Find your own concept per video.** The list below is what the reference did, as examples. Do not pick from it; invent what fits this video's ideas, and avoid the last explainer's concepts for the same brand (`concepts.md`).

## Look
- **Canvas**: near-white (faint square grid, a few soft blurred glows in brand navy and amber). Calm, never busy. Photos sit on it as cards; full-bleed footage is the exception for emotional shots.
- **Palette roles** (from the reference; real values come from `brand`): navy/indigo = headlines, key numbers, dark cards; amber/orange = highlight word, underline, check marks, accents, counters; white = cards; muted grey-violet = secondary text. One accent per screen: amber marks the single word or number that matters.
- **Cards**: white, large corner radius (about 24-32 px at 1080p), a soft diffuse shadow with a faint indigo tint, no hard outline. Dark navy cards for the one "hero" block per scene. Small amber-tinted pills (rounded capsule, dot + text) name the topic of the scene at the top.
- **Type scale** (1080p): hero number 200+ px bold; headline 70-90 px bold navy with one amber word; card title 40-50 px; body 28-34 px; captions/labels 20-26 px. Single sans family (Inter-like), bold for titles, medium for body. Never below about 20 px at 1080p. Vietnamese diacritics need generous line height.
- **Logo**: top-left, same spot in every scene, small, on a white rounded plate when over footage; full-size and centered only for the opening and closing.
- **Footage on screen**: inside a card, or full-frame with a lower label chip; tagged "Minh họa" when it is not the real thing.

## Motion
- **Build-up**: elements enter one by one with the voice (list items, steps, bars), each with a short ease-out and small travel; the viewer is never shown the finished page first.
- **Morphs, not cuts**: a card becomes the next layout (photo shrinks to a corner and a panel grows beside it; a title slides up into the pill). No hard cut to a box; transitions are soft and continuous.
- **Numbers count up**; progress bars fill; a cursor or highlight points at the row being discussed.
- **Emphasis**: highlighter underline sweeps under the key phrase; one thing moves at a time.
- **Holds**: let the final state rest long enough to read the text twice.
- Time motion on the voice (`at`/`until`), not on guessed seconds.

## The reference's concepts (examples only)
1. Cards of campus photos drop into a grid that spells "13 cơ sở".
2. Benefit checklist on a frosted panel over classroom footage.
3. Giant "1-1" on a navy field, then circles marking teacher and student on footage.
4. Numbered step cards (01, 03) with icon beside the footage.
5. Skill-vs-sessions curve with small photos pinned to points.
6. Progress track "Đang học / Vững dần / Thành thạo" with sparkles at the end.
7. A prompt typed into an AI chat box, courses fanning out from it.
8. A syllabus sheet unfolding into a library, plus a before/during/after timeline.
9. 3D region discs with yellow pins (one pin = one campus) and a count table.
10. Morning/afternoon/evening timeline over a sky that changes colour.
11. Weekly calendar grids per student type.
12. Certificate folder plus three certificate cards joined by a 3-step arrow.
13. Rating table whose numbers count up, ending on one floating total card.
14. Review wall that zooms into each quote and underlines its key phrase.
15. A chat-style "advisor" panel that picks the learner's route, then a grid of benefit tiles and the logo ending.

## Check before showing a still
Text readable at phone size; one idea per page; logo in the same place; no more than one amber accent; nothing important in the platform's unsafe zone; footage real, not stock-looking; no concept copied from the list above or from the brand's previous video.
